Section 1

What is ParaIQ?

ParaIQ is a secure web-based platform that helps law firms manage cases, review documents, and use AI to speed up legal work — all in one place.

Think of it as a digital filing cabinet, a smart document reviewer, and a case management system in one. You use it in a normal web browser — no software to install.


What you can do:

  • Keep all case files organized by matter
  • Have AI highlight important people, dates, and legal terms in documents
  • Track which documents are privileged
  • Manage discovery, depositions, motions, and contracts
  • Export reports as PDFs or Word files
Good to know
ParaIQ works in Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. No installation needed.

Section 4

Working with matters

A matter is ParaIQ's word for a case or legal project. Everything is linked to a matter.

1
Click Matters in the sidebar to see all matters you have access to.
2
Click any matter name to open it and see all documents, notes, and activity.
3
Use the search bar to find a matter by client name or case number.
Tip
If you do not see a New Matter button, your role does not have that permission. Contact your administrator.

Section 8

Discovery

Manage document productions, requests, and responses — organized by matter.

1
Click Discovery and select the matter from the dropdown.
2
Use the tabs: Productions, Requests, and Responses.
3
ParaIQ automatically checks the privilege log before including any document in a production.
Tip
Use the Bates numbering feature to add sequential document numbers before finalizing any production.
Section 9

Depositions

Store transcripts and prepare using AI-assisted summarization.

1
Click Depositions in the sidebar.
2
Click New Deposition and fill in the witness name, date, and matter.
3
Upload the transcript, then click Summarize for a plain-English AI summary.
4
Always verify important statements against the original transcript.
Tip
Use the search bar inside a deposition to find any word or phrase instantly.
